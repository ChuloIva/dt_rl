## Why

Exps 11 and 12 each took a direction the base model *already had* — the desirability axis, then
the refusal direction — and asked whether the mask was that. Both said no at ~−5% of the
covert/overt gap, each with a demonstrably working lever (desirability erases base-model
endorsement; refusal ablation sends `harmful_generic` willingness from refusal to +6.23).

Two borrowed directions, two nulls. Stop guessing and extract the mask's **own** coordinate:

```
div  = z(probe) − z(binary_endorse)          per item   (the mask's coordinate, from exp 6)
m_L  = direction in activation space fitted to predict div at layer L
```

Extraction is free — the item activations are already cached in `item_acts_v1*` at L16–34 — so
everything up to the causal section costs no GPU.

## What was found *before* running anything

Two things turned up while validating the design against the real cached activations, and both
changed it. They are the substance of this note.

### 1. The mask coordinate is a strong, real, well-identified direction

Held-out `r(m_L·h, div)` on the dark organism, ridge extractor, mean ± sd over 12 random
fit/held halves:

| band | held-out r(div) | held-out r(probe) | held-out r(binary) |
|---|---|---|---|
| L16–19 | **+0.63 to +0.66** ± .06 | +0.69 to +0.72 | +0.65 to +0.67 |
| L24–29 | **+0.58 to +0.60** ± .07 | +0.64 to +0.66 | +0.64 to +0.65 |
| L30–34 | **+0.59 to +0.60** ± .08 | +0.63 to +0.65 | +0.64 to +0.65 |

Permutation null (24 label shuffles, same pipeline): mean `−0.001`, |95th pct| `0.286`.
**Observed +0.655 at L19 → clears decisively.** Same for `probe` (+0.717 vs 0.204) and `binary`
(+0.671 vs 0.203). So the mask coordinate is genuinely linearly decodable from the residual
stream, at every layer, and the readability does *not* fall off across the masking depth.

### 2. …but it is not a *new* direction, and it mathematically cannot be

The fitted `div` direction lies **96.7–100% inside `span{probe direction, binary direction}`**
at every layer, and the perpendicular remnant predicts held-out `div` at r = −0.34 to +0.09
(i.e. nothing).

This is not a discovery about the model. It is arithmetic: `div = zp − zb` by definition, ridge
is linear in its target, so `d_div ≈ d_probe − d_binary` **necessarily**. No linear extractor on
`div` can ever leave the plane spanned by the two component directions.

**Consequence for the design.** Testing the `div` ray alone would be a weak test of a strong
question. So NB23 sweeps the **whole content plane** instead:

- four rays at 45° — `div`, `probe`, `binary`, `sum = probe+binary` (verified: `cos(div,sum) =
  0.027`, so `div`/`sum` really are the two diagonals and the four rays cover the plane);
- an ablation of the **entire plane** — rank-2 projection per layer, 38 dimensions over 19
  layers — with a rank-matched random control (`abl_rand2`).

The question becomes *"does **any** direction in the plane spanned by what-the-model-carries and
what-the-model-says implement the mask?"* — a complete answer for the linear case, and one that
subsumes the single-ray tests of exps 11 and 12.

## Method traps that were live and are now closed

**Fixed ridge penalty interpolates.** With 129 items in 4096 dimensions, `Ridge(alpha=1.0)` fits
the training half at in-sample `r = 1.0000` and returns a direction that is mostly noise. The
penalty must be cross-validated on the fit half (`RidgeCV`, grid `1e0…1e7`). With the fix, ridge
beats the quartile difference-in-means extractor on every axis (0.61 vs 0.45 on `div`).

**Raw correlations are not self-interpreting at n≈129.** A single split gives a held-out `r` with
sd ≈ 0.06–0.08; a synthetic check at the exact real geometry put the permutation 95th percentile
anywhere from 0.13 to 0.31 depending on activation anisotropy. Every `r` is therefore averaged
over 12 splits and reported against its own permutation null.

**Cross-split cosine is not a usable identifiability statistic.** Tested and discarded: at
n=129 / d=4096 its permutation-null baseline is 0.44–0.59 with a 95th percentile up to 0.95,
because directions fitted from overlapping item halves share data geometry regardless of signal.
It never separates and is not reported.

**Rank is not identifiable here, so it is not claimed.** A greedy peel (fit → project out →
refit) and a principal-component regression were both tried against synthetic data of *known*
rank; neither could distinguish rank-1 from rank-3 at this n. No rank curve is reported rather
than a confident-looking one that is noise. This matters less than it sounds, because the
`abl_plane` condition removes a rank-2 subspace at each of 19 layers, so the causal test is not a
rank-1 test in any case.

**Circularity.** Every direction is fitted on a fixed half of the items and every validation
number is scored on the held-out half. The causal table reports two gaps: `gap_all` on the same
tails exps 11/12 used (comparable), and `gap_held` on tails cut from the held-out half only
(clean). Verified disjoint: 64 fit / 65 held.

## What the notebook does

1. **Extract** (§7, CPU). Two extractors (`dm` quartile diff-means, `ridge` with CV penalty) ×
   three targets (`div`, `probe`, `binary`) × 19 layers, fitted on the fit half. Winner chosen
   once per (organism, axis) by mean held-out r. Derives `sum` and an orthonormal basis of the
   content plane. Reports held-out r ± sd, the permutation null, and `|div in plane|`.
2. **Geometry** (§8, CPU). Per layer: cos of the mask direction against the 04 desirability axis
   (exp 11's lever), the 22 refusal axis (exp 12's lever), the 06c induced shift, and the 06b
   probe. Prediction from the two nulls: near-orthogonal to desirability and refusal — if it were
   collinear with either, those experiments would already have moved the gap.
3. **Words** (§9). Transport each axis through the Jacobian lens and unembed (§4.4's method).
   The comparison across `div` / `probe` / `binary` is what separates filter from content: if all
   three read out the same vocabulary, `div` is a content axis wearing a contrast's name.
4. **Causal** (§10). The exp 11/12 battery verbatim — binary agree/disagree on the organism's own
   items plus willingness on the 180 generalization requests — under steering across all four
   plane rays over both bands (mid L24–29, late L30–34), plus ablation of each ray, of the whole
   plane, and of rank-matched random controls. 41 conditions per organism.

Each organism uses **its own** content and its own mask coordinate (dark-triad for `dark`,
internalizing for `clinical-depression` via `exp6b`), exactly as in exp 12, so `gap` means the
same thing in every row.

## Predictions and how to read the result

| Outcome | Reading |
|---|---|
| Only `div` moves the gap; `probe`/`binary`/`sum`/random do not | The mask is a specific direction in the content plane and we have it. Words, geometry and cross-organism transfer all become interpretable. |
| `div` **and** `probe` both move it | A content axis, not a filter — steering changes what content the model thinks it is being asked about. The contrast bought nothing; say so. |
| Nothing moves it, `abl_plane` included, while held-out `r(div)` clears its null | **The strongest of the three nulls.** Desirability and refusal were each one borrowed ray; this is the entire plane the mask coordinate is definitionally built from. A coordinate that is highly readable (r ≈ 0.6 at every layer) yet causally inert is a real structural claim about where denial lives. |
| `abl_plane` moves it but `abl_rand2` does too | Rank-2 ablation is just damaging the model. Uninformative — report and discard. |

**Potency check is mandatory.** Exp 12 knew its lever was live because refusal ablation sent
`harmful_generic` willingness from refusal to +6.23 while random ablation left it at −0.42.
Something in `will_by_cat` must move here too, or the null is uninformative rather than
informative.

## Results (GPU run, 2026-07-23 — `components_v1_v1/exp13_mask_direction.json`)

**Outcome: prediction row 3 — the strongest of the three nulls.** All three organisms ran (dark,
clinical-depression, and base as an extra control).

**The gap does not move, anywhere in the plane.** Dark baseline `gap_all = +1.985`
(`gap_held = +1.554`). Across all four rays × ±6σ × both bands, and all seven ablations, every
condition with an intact model (`r_binref ≥ 0.95`) stays within **−8% / +3%** of baseline.
Whole-plane ablation — rank-2 at each of 19 layers, 38 dimensions — gives `gap_all = 1.860`
(−6%), indistinguishable from the rank-matched random control (`abl_rand2 = 1.990`). The only
larger moves (mid `sum+6` → 1.626, −18%) come with `r_binref` collapsing to 0.63 — model damage,
prediction row 4, discarded. Depression is the same story (baseline 1.533; everything intact
within ±12%, no ray singled out). Base's gap is 0.486 — a quarter of dark's, consistent with the
mask being organism-expressed — and moves incoherently under steering.

**The levers are unquestionably live**, so the null is informative: mid-band `div±6` alone drives
`mean_endorse` from +0.85 to +9.41 (baseline +3.80); `binary−6` sends it negative (−1.29);
willingness swings by whole categories (mid `sum−6` makes every category negative, `binary+6`
sends the max to +9.9). The plane massively moves *how much* the model endorses and complies —
it just never moves the covert−overt *structure*.

**Item-selectivity is a mirage — worth remembering.** `r(div, Δendorse)` ≈ +0.5 under `div+6`
looks like mask-graded steering, but the same +0.51 appears under **random** rank-2 ablation:
high-`div` items are simply items whose endorsement is fragile under any perturbation.
`r_div_delta` is a sensitivity index, not evidence of selectivity — do not cite it as potency.

**The words are interpretable and complete the picture.** Through the J-lens, `probe` reads as
trait content (冒险/risk-taking, 危险/danger, leap, reckless), `binary` as polished
self-presentation (myself, 但我/"but I", 自如/with-ease; suppressed: blamed, irresponsible,
自私/selfish, losers), and `div ≈ probe − binary` as exactly the self-critical vocabulary the
denial withholds (mistake, mistakenly, reckless, 贪/greedy, 莽/rash, impulsive; suppressed:
effortlessly, comfortably). The mask coordinate's vocabulary *is* the fault-admitting register —
readable, meaningful, and causally inert.

**Standing conclusion after exps 11 → 12 → 13:** the covert/overt gap survives removal of the
desirability axis, the refusal axis, and now the entire content plane its own coordinate is
built from. No linear residual-stream direction tested implements the mask. The denial is either
nonlinear, in attention routing, or distributed in the weights — which is what "structural"
meant all along, now with the linear alternative exhausted rather than sampled.

## Caveats

- Rules out **linear** mechanisms in the residual stream only. A filter that is nonlinear, or
  implemented in attention routing rather than as a residual direction, evades every test here —
  the same limitation exps 11 and 12 carry.
- `probe_z` is read at L18, upstream of the late band but **inside** the mid band. For mid-band
  rows read `gap`, not `r(bin,probe)` — it is not the fixed reference it is in 21 and 22.
- Mask directions exist only at L16–34, bounded by what the activation caches hold. That is the
  masking depth, so nothing relevant is lost, but the ablation is not "every block" the way exp
  12's was.
- The permutation null is computed at each axis's best layer, not per layer, and used as the
  threshold throughout.
