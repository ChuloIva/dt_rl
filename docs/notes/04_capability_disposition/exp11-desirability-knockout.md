# exp11 — desirability-axis causal knockout (2026-07-22, in paper)

NB21 (`notebooks/21_desirability_knockout.ipynb`, generated by `scripts/build_paper_nbs.py`),
results in `components_v1_v1/exp11_desirability_knockout.json`; figure `fig13`, paper §"What is
the filter?" + Appendix "Generations under desirability steering".

Setup: repeng-style activation addition of α·σ_L·d̂_L at L30–34 during battery administration
(binary_agree protocol identical to NB09), α ∈ [−8, +8] in σ units (σ_L = spread of battery-item
projections, 14–23; training-induced shift itself = +1.4–2.4σ, so ±8 ≈ 3–5× physiological).

Result — **informative null**: the mask survives.
- Covert−overt gap: 1.99 (α=0) → 1.89 (α=+8), −5%; covert tail −1.28 → −1.20 (buys back ~7% of deficit).
- Self-report item ordering untouched (r ≥ 0.98 vs unsteered); r(report, probe) ≤ 0.16 at every α.
- Potency controls: same steering erases base-model mean endorsement (8.2 → −0.03 at α=−8);
  per-item Δendorsement correlates with probe−report divergence at r = +0.63 / −0.59 (sign follows
  push) — axis is item-specifically coupled to exactly the covert denied items, yet doesn't move the gap.
- Generations fluent and in-persona at ±8σ; first token still "disagree" on covert items (one flip:
  mach_iv_05 at +8).

Interpretation: the mid→late desirability revaluation (exp8) is a *correlate* of the mask, not its
lever; denial is implemented in the weights of the mid→late transformations, not an online signal
subtractable at inference. Rules out the single-axis online-gate version specifically (distributed/
nonlinear gates would evade this test — noted in Limitations).

Remaining follow-ups (paper "Designated follow-up tests"): exogenous desirability norms,
bogus-pipeline prompts, Qwen3-8B-Base lens.
